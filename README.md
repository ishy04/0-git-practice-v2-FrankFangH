# DeepSeek

## [What to Know About DeepSeek and How It Is Upending A.I.](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/27/technology/what-is-deepseek-china-ai.html)

DeepSeek's technique involves a method known as the "mixture of experts," which optimizes the use of specialized AI models to perform tasks more efficiently by distributing data analysis across them. This method significantly reduces the need for vast computational power by minimizing the data transfer times between models — a common efficiency bottleneck in traditional setups.

eg. The world’s top companies typically train their chatbots with supercomputers that use as many as 16,000 chips or more. DeepSeek’s engineers said they needed only about 2,000 Nvidia chips.
